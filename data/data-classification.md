# Data Classification

## Levels

Public data can be shared freely. Internal data is the default for day to day
work. Confidential data covers customer records, contracts and anything
covered by a non disclosure agreement. Restricted data covers credentials,
personal identifiers and payment data.

## Handling rules

Confidential and restricted data must stay inside approved systems. Do not
copy it to personal storage, personal email or an unapproved artificial
intelligence tool.

## Sharing externally

External sharing of confidential data requires a signed non disclosure
agreement and a link with an expiry date. Anonymous links are disabled for
confidential and restricted content.

## Retention

Customer data is retained for the period stated in the contract and is deleted
within 30 days of that period ending unless a legal hold applies.
