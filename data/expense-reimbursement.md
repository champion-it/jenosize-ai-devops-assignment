# Expense Reimbursement

## Submitting a claim

Claims are submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense.
Attach a readable receipt. A card statement on its own is not accepted as
evidence.

## Approval and payment

Your manager approves claims under the departmental limit. Anything above it
also needs finance approval. Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run,
which closes on the 25th of each month.

## Travel

Economy class is the standard for flights under six hours. Accommodation is
booked through the corporate travel agent so that duty of care tracking works.

## What cannot be claimed

Fines, personal entertainment and alcohol outside a client meal are not
reimbursable.
