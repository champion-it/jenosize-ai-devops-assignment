# Laptop Request and Replacement

## Standard hardware

The standard issue is a 14 inch laptop with 32 GB of memory. Engineering roles
that build container images locally can request the 64 GB variant with manager
approval.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops are refreshed every three years. You receive a notification 30 days
before your device becomes eligible and can schedule the swap yourself.

## Damaged or lost devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop immediately through the service desk hotline so
the device can be remotely wiped. Do not wait until the next business day.
A replacement is issued within two business days.

## Returning a device

Devices are returned at the end of the refresh or when leaving the company.
Back up anything you need to the corporate drive first. Returned devices are
wiped to the standard erasure policy before being redeployed or recycled.
