# Offboarding Checklist

## Notice period

The manager raises the offboarding request as soon as the leaving date is
confirmed. Human resources and the service desk work from the same request.

## Last day

Accounts are disabled at the end of the last working day rather than deleted,
so that mailbox content remains available to the manager for 90 days. Hardware
is returned to the service desk and building access is revoked.

## Data handover

Transfer ownership of documents and repositories before the last day. Content
stored only on the laptop is lost when the device is wiped.

## Contractors

Contractor accounts expire automatically on the contract end date. Extending a
contract requires the account expiry to be updated, otherwise access stops
overnight.
