# Incident Reporting

## What to report

Report anything that affects availability, integrity or confidentiality of a
service or its data. A suspected phishing message, an unexpected password
prompt and a production outage are all incidents.

## How to report

Use the incident hotline for anything urgent. The portal is appropriate for
issues that can wait until the next business day. Include what you observed,
when it started and which systems are affected.

## Severity levels

Severity one covers a complete outage of a customer facing service or a
confirmed data breach, and pages the on call engineer immediately. Severity
two covers degraded service with a workaround. Severity three covers
everything else.

## After an incident

Severity one and two incidents get a blameless postmortem within five business
days. The postmortem records the timeline, the contributing factors and the
follow up actions with owners.
