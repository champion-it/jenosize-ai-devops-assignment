# Onboarding Checklist

## Before the first day

The hiring manager raises the onboarding request at least five business days
before the start date so that the account, laptop and building access are
ready. Late requests are the most common cause of a new joiner spending the
first day without a working account.

## First day

Collect the laptop from the service desk, sign in, enrol in multi factor
authentication and complete the security awareness module. The module must be
finished within the first week.

## First week

Your manager assigns a buddy and grants the team specific access you need.
Access to production systems is never granted on day one and requires the
on call training to be completed first.
