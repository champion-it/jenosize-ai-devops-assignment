# Email Setup

## Adding your mailbox on a laptop

The mail client on a managed laptop is configured automatically when you sign
in for the first time. No server names need to be entered manually.

## Adding your mailbox on a phone

Install the corporate mail application from the company portal rather than
using the built in mail app. The corporate application enforces device
encryption and remote wipe, which the built in client does not support.

## Mailbox quotas

The standard mailbox quota is 50 GB. At 90 percent you receive a warning and
at 100 percent outgoing mail is blocked while incoming mail continues to be
delivered for seven days. Archive older items to reclaim space rather than
requesting a quota increase.

## Shared mailboxes

Shared mailbox access is requested by the mailbox owner, not by the person who
needs access. Access is reviewed every six months.
