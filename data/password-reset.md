# Password Reset

## Resetting a forgotten password

Open the self service portal at https://id.jenosize.example/reset and enter
your work email address. A reset link is sent to your registered recovery
address and stays valid for 15 minutes. The link can only be used once.

If the reset email does not arrive within five minutes, check the junk folder
before raising a ticket. Mail filtering quarantines the message occasionally
when the recovery address is a personal mailbox.

## Password requirements

A new password must be at least 14 characters long and must not match any of
your previous five passwords. Passphrases of three or more unrelated words are
accepted and are easier to remember than substitutions such as replacing the
letter o with a zero.

## Account lockout

Five consecutive failed sign in attempts lock the account for 30 minutes. The
service desk can clear a lockout immediately after verifying your identity
over a video call. Lockouts are logged and reviewed weekly.
