# Printer Troubleshooting

## Adding a printer

Printers are published by floor. Add one from the company portal and choose
the printer that matches the label on the device. Drivers install
automatically.

## Jobs stuck in the queue

A job that stays in the queue is usually waiting for release at the device.
Badge in at the printer within four hours or the job is deleted automatically.

## Print quality problems

Streaks across a page normally mean the drum needs replacing rather than the
toner. Report the printer asset number so facilities can order the right part.

## Scanning to email

Scan to email sends to your own address only. The size limit per scan is
25 MB, which is roughly 40 pages at the default resolution.
